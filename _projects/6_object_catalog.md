**Instructor:** Prof. Pratik Narang, BITS Pilani

**Code:** [github.com/Ash0271/everyday-object-catalog](https://github.com/Ash0271/everyday-object-catalog)

Built a lightweight multi-task image understanding system on a self-collected object dataset. I fine-tuned **DeiT-Tiny** and **MobileViT** backbones for joint classification, multi-attribute prediction and text-to-image retrieval, and compared performance across dataset sizes to study data-efficiency trade-offs.

**Tools:** PyTorch, timm, Hugging Face
