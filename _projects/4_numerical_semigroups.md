### Why pure mathematics

Alongside applied machine learning, I wanted a foundation in theory and in writing rigorous proofs.

### The problem

A numerical semigroup is a set of non-negative integers closed under addition, generated here by three consecutive triangular numbers:

$$S = \langle T_n, T_{n+1}, T_{n+2} \rangle, \qquad T_n = \tfrac{n(n+1)}{2}.$$

We studied its structural properties, in particular its **isolated gaps** (numbers missing from the semigroup whose neighbors are both in it).

### How we worked

We first observed properties computationally, using GAP and Python, and then proved them. Weekly sessions with my collaborator and supervisor went into checking edge cases and tightening arguments.

### My contribution

For one of our key results, I developed a new proof, building on an idea from an earlier paper we had studied. My supervisor adopted it into our manuscript, which is now being finalized for submission.
