Singapore

Hi, I’m Anshuman (Ash). I’m a final-year dual-degree student in Mathematics (MSc) and Computer Science (BE) at BITS Pilani, India.

I’m interested in the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence: building models that learn the way brains do, and using those models to understand the brain.

I’m currently doing my undergraduate thesis with Prof. Camilo Libedinsky at the National University of Singapore, where I study whether an agent, like an animal, can learn to regulate its internal state when surprising, need-relevant experiences shape what it remembers. Before that, I was an IUSSTF–Viterbi Fellow (2025) at the University of Southern California, where I built a hybrid vision transformer to predict brain age from structural MRI in Prof. Andrei Irimia’s group.

At BITS, I have also worked on the security of federated learning, numerical semigroups in pure mathematics, and solar irradiance forecasting. I’ve served as a Teaching Assistant and Lead Teaching Assistant for courses in BITS Pilani’s online BSc Computer Science program.

I am applying to PhD programs starting in Fall 2027.
